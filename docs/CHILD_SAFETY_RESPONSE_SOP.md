# Child Safety Response SOP

Private internal procedure. Do not publish this document as the public-facing
child-safety standard. It contains no case data, credentials, or secrets.

## Scope and ownership

This procedure covers reports or other credible notices involving child sexual
abuse or exploitation (CSAE), including suspected child sexual abuse material
(CSAM), on Remdy. The designated contact is `contact@remdy.app`. The person
assigned to that mailbox must be able to explain Remdy's review and enforcement
process and coordinate action.

Remdy is intended only for adults aged 18 or older. That restriction does not
reduce the priority of a child-safety report or remove the need to follow this
procedure.

## 1. Priority intake and triage

1. Treat every report with reason code
   `child_sexual_exploitation_or_abuse` as critical priority.
2. Limit initial access to personnel assigned to safety review.
3. Confirm the report references, affected account or content identifiers,
   timestamps, and reporter context. Do not duplicate suspected illegal media.
4. Assess whether a child or another person may be in immediate danger. If so,
   escalate immediately under the emergency section below.
5. Record the intake time, reviewer, decisions, and actions in the restricted
   case record. Do not include unnecessary personal data.

## 2. Immediate restriction

When necessary to prevent harm or redistribution, promptly restrict access to
the reported content or account while review is pending. Use the least action
that safely contains the risk, which may include hiding content, disabling
sharing, suspending an account, or terminating access.

Do not notify a reported user in a way that could endanger a child, compromise
an investigation, or cause destruction of evidence.

## 3. Secure preservation

- Preserve only information necessary for investigation, legal compliance, and
  enforcement.
- Keep preserved material in access-controlled systems with access logging.
- Do not download, forward, screenshot, or copy suspected CSAM into tickets,
  email, chat, test fixtures, personal devices, or new storage locations.
- Preserve original identifiers, storage references, timestamps, account IDs,
  report metadata, and relevant audit records when lawful and necessary.
- Follow applicable legal retention and preservation requirements. Seek
  qualified legal advice when scope or jurisdiction is unclear.

## 4. Review and enforcement

1. Review the report and available lawful evidence using restricted access.
2. Remove or disable access to violating content after obtaining actual
   knowledge, consistent with applicable law and the public Remdy standards.
3. Suspend or terminate accounts involved in CSAE or CSAM as appropriate.
4. Disable known sharing paths and references to prevent redistribution without
   creating new copies of the material.
5. Check related reports and identifiers only to the extent necessary and
   authorized.
6. Record the factual basis, reviewer, timestamps, actions, and final decision.

## 5. Reporting to competent authorities

Confirmed CSAM or other reportable child exploitation must be referred to the
National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or the competent
regional or national authority when required by applicable law. Determine the
correct reporting body and required information for the relevant jurisdiction.

Remdy does not currently claim an automated integration with NCMEC or any law
enforcement authority. An authorized, trained person must make and document the
required referral through the authority's official channel.

## 6. Immediate danger

If the available information indicates an imminent threat to a child or another
person, contact the appropriate local emergency or law-enforcement authority
without waiting for the ordinary review cycle. Provide only information that is
lawful, necessary, accurate, and securely handled.

The in-app confirmation advises reporters to contact local authorities when
there is immediate danger; this does not replace Remdy's own escalation duties.

## 7. Case closure and follow-up

Before closure:

- confirm content restriction or removal is complete;
- confirm account enforcement is complete;
- confirm redistribution paths identified in the review are disabled;
- confirm any legally required report was submitted and logged;
- document the decision and evidence basis without unnecessary sensitive data;
- set an authorized retention or deletion date for the restricted case record;
- review whether product, detection, policy, or training improvements are
  needed; and
- close the case only after a second authorized review for confirmed CSAE or
  CSAM.

Review this SOP regularly and whenever law, Google Play policy, reporting
channels, or Remdy's product capabilities change.
